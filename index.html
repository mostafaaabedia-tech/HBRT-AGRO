Your greenhouse, from anywhere.

Live readings and full control over the internet. No tunnel, no port forwarding.

  1. Flash firmware 5.0 to the greenhouse ESP32.
  2. In the HBRT AGRO app open Settings, then Share access link. Or open the board's address on its Wi-Fi and tap Open from anywhere.
  3. Open that link here, or paste it below.
